# Ecommerce Product Photography on a Budget

You're bootstrapping. Every dollar counts. But bad product photos kill conversions. Here's how to get professional-looking images at every budget level.

## Level 0: $0—Your Phone + AI

**What you need:**
- Your smartphone camera
- Natural window light
- A white piece of paper or poster board
- EcomPic AI (free tier)

**Process:**
1. Place product on white paper near a window
2. Take a photo with your phone
3. Upload to EcomPic → one-click white background
4. Generate lifestyle scenes as needed
5. Download in platform sizes

**Result:** Amazon-compliant images that look professional enough to start selling immediately.

## Level 1: $50—Basic Setup

**What you add:**
- Lightbox kit ($30 on Amazon)
- Mini tripod ($15)
- Remains: AI for post-processing

With a lightbox, you get consistent lighting regardless of weather. This eliminates the biggest variable in DIY photography. Combined with AI for backgrounds and scenes, this setup produces images indistinguishable from professional studio work.

## Level 2: $200—Enhanced Setup

**What you add:**
- Entry-level DSLR or mirrorless camera (used)
- Two softbox lights ($40 each)
- Boom arm for overhead shots ($30)

**But here's the thing:** At this level, your bottleneck isn't equipment—it's time. Even with great gear, editing 50 product photos takes hours. This is where AI pays for itself: you shoot raw photos, AI handles all editing.

## Level 3: $500+/month—"Professional"

**What you get:**
- Professional photographer
- Studio rental
- 2-3 day turnaround per batch

**The truth:** Most sellers at this level are overpaying. The same results are achievable at Level 0-1 with AI. The only exception is products requiring extremely precise color accuracy (luxury watches, high-end cosmetics).

## The Math

Let's say you have 100 SKUs and need 5 images each (500 total):

| Method | Cost | Time |
|--------|------|------|
| Professional photographer | $2,500-5,000 | 1-2 weeks |
| DIY + Lightroom | Your time (50+ hours) | Forever |
| Phone + AI | $0-19 | 2 hours |
