## Step 1: Take a Basic Photo

You just need one decent photo of your product. Use your phone. Here's how:

- Place the product on a plain surface
- Use natural light (near a window)
- Hold steady or use a $10 tripod
- Take multiple angles

The photo doesn't need to be perfect. AI handles the rest.
