# Shopify Product Photography Guide: AI-Powered Tips for 2026

Shopify powers over 4 million online stores. Standing out means having product photos that stop the scroll. Here's your complete Shopify product photography guide — updated for the AI era.

## Shopify Image Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| Format | JPEG, PNG, or WebP |
| Maximum size | 20 MB per image |
| Resolution | Up to 4472 x 4472 px |
| Recommended | 2048 x 2048 px for zoom |
| Color space | sRGB |

Shopify automatically compresses and optimizes images, so upload at the highest quality you have. The platform handles responsive resizing.

## Types of Product Photos Every Shopify Store Needs

### 1. Main Product Shot (White Background)
The clean, distraction-free shot that shows exactly what you're selling. This is the image that appears in search results, collection pages, and the product page hero.

**How to create it with AI**: Upload a basic phone photo. AI removes the background and places your product on pure white. Done in seconds.

### 2. Lifestyle / In-Context Photos
Show your product being used. A coffee mug on a desk. A lamp in a living room. A bag carried on a city street. Lifestyle photos help customers imagine ownership.

**How to create it with AI**: Upload your product photo, describe the scene, and AI generates a photorealistic lifestyle image with your actual product.

### 3. Detail / Close-Up Shots
Zoom in on texture, materials, stitching, or unique features. Detail shots reduce return rates by setting accurate expectations.

### 4. Scale & Size Reference
Help customers understand dimensions. A wallet next to a phone. A plant next to a chair. Size complaints are among the top reasons for returns.

### 5. Color Variants
If you sell in multiple colors, show every option. AI can recolor products without re-photographing each variant.

## Pro Tips

- **Use consistent aspect ratios** across your catalog (1:1 square recommended)
- **Optimize alt text** — "Blue ceramic coffee mug on wooden desk" beats "IMG_4582.jpg"
- **Test with real shoppers** — run A/B tests on your top 5 products
- **Refresh seasonally** — new images signal active management to both customers and Google
