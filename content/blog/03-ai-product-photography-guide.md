# AI Product Photography—The Complete Guide

AI image generation has matured from a novelty to a production-ready tool. Here's how ecommerce sellers are using it to compete with brands that have 10x their budget.

## What AI Product Photography Actually Does

Instead of taking photos in a studio, you feed an existing photo to an AI model and tell it what you want. The AI understands your product and generates a new image that keeps your product intact while changing everything else.

**What it can do:**

- Remove backgrounds and replace with pure white
- Place your product in realistic lifestyle scenes
- Change colors, materials, and finishes
- Add text, labels, and infographic elements
- Generate product photos from text descriptions alone
- Resize and format for any marketplace

**What it can't do (yet):**

- Perfectly preserve tiny text on product labels
- Guarantee 100% identical product geometry in every generation
- Replace physical samples for quality control
