# Social Media Product Images That Convert: Instagram, Pinterest & TikTok Guide

Social media sells products. But the images that work on Amazon don't work on Instagram. Each platform has its own visual language, aspect ratio, and audience expectation. Here's how to optimize product images for every social platform — without re-shooting anything.

## Platform-by-Platform Image Specs

| Platform | Best Ratio | Resolution | Format |
|----------|-----------|------------|--------|
| Instagram Feed | 1:1 (square) or 4:5 | 1080 x 1350 | JPEG |
| Instagram Stories | 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 | JPEG |
| Pinterest | 2:3 (vertical) | 1000 x 1500 | JPEG / PNG |
| TikTok / Reels | 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 | JPEG (thumbnail) / MP4 |
| Facebook Ads | 1:1 or 4:5 | 1080 x 1080 or 1200 x 1500 | JPEG |
| Twitter/X | 16:9 or 1:1 | 1200 x 675 or 1080 x 1080 | JPEG |

## Instagram: Aspirational & Lifestyle-First

Instagram isn't a catalog — it's a mood board. Product images here should feel editorial, not transactional.

**Feed Post Strategy:**
- **70% lifestyle content** — products in beautiful settings, being used and enjoyed
- **20% educational** — tips, how-tos, behind-the-scenes process
- **10% promotional** — sales, launches, "link in bio"

**How AI Creates Instagram Content:**
- Generate lifestyle scenes of your product in aspirational settings
- Create seasonal variations — summer picnics, winter coziness, fall aesthetics
- Produce flat lays with complementary props
- Generate carousel-ready before/after sequences

**Instagram Pro Tip**: Carousel posts (multiple images) get 3x more engagement than single-image posts. Create a 5-10 image carousel showing your product from different angles, in different settings, with different use cases.

## Pinterest: Search Engine for Inspiration

Pinterest users are planners and shoppers. They search with intent — "kitchen remodel ideas," "fall outfit inspiration," "nursery decor." Your product images need to fit into their vision.

**Pinterest Image Rules:**
- **Vertical only** (2:3 ratio, 1000x1500px minimum)
- **Text overlay recommended** — "10 Cozy Fall Decorating Ideas" outperforms untitled images
- **Rich, warm colors** beat sterile white backgrounds
- **No faces** — faceless imagery performs better on Pinterest
- **Brand logo small and subtle** — bottom of the image

**How AI Creates Pinterest Content:**
- Generate vertical lifestyle scenes — your product in curated Pinterest-worthy settings
- Create collage-style pins with text overlays
- Produce "Idea Pin" sequences (video + images mixed)
- Seasonal board refreshes without re-shooting

**Pinterest SEO Tip**: Your pin's alt text and description function like Google SEO. Use keywords naturally: "Handmade ceramic mug styled on rustic wooden coffee table with morning light."

## TikTok & Reels: Quick, Authentic, Demo-Driven

Short-form video is the highest-engagement product format right now. But thumbnails and cover images still matter — they're what stops the scroll before the video plays.

**Product Image Approach for TikTok:**
- Less polished than Instagram — authentic > perfect
- Show the product in action (before/after, transformation)
- Use trending formats (unboxing, ASMR, comparison)
- Bright, high-contrast thumbnails

**How AI Helps:**
- Generate eye-catching cover thumbnails (9:16 vertical)
- Create before/after comparison images for video intros
- Produce product flat lays for demonstration pins
