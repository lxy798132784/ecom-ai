## What AI Doesn't Replace (Yet)

- **Highly complex product interactions** (pouring liquid, kinetic shots)
- **Brand-defining creative direction** (AI is a tool, not an art director)
- **Authentic human lifestyle photography** with real models (but AI hybrid approaches are closing the gap)
- **360° spin photography** (though AI-assisted solutions are emerging)

## The Smart Hybrid Approach

The winning strategy for 2026: **AI for volume, photoshoot for hero content.**

- Use AI for your entire catalog (white background + standard lifestyle scenes)
- Invest in a premium photoshoot for your top 5 best-selling products
- Use AI to extend the photoshoot (recolor, rescale, regenerate backgrounds)
- Refresh seasonally with AI while reusing base photos

This gives you the best of both worlds: elite visuals for your money-makers, affordable coverage for everything else.
