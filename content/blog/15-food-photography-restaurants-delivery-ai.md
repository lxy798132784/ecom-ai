# Food Photography for Restaurants and Delivery Apps: AI-Powered Guide

Menu items with photos sell 30% more than items without. On delivery apps, restaurants with professional food images see 25-40% higher order rates. But professional food photography costs $200-500 per dish. Here's how AI changes the equation.

## Why Food Photography Is Different

Food photography is the hardest product category. Food wilts, melts, steams, and dies under hot lights. A burger looks perfect for 90 seconds. Ice cream, for about 20 seconds before the scoop slides off the cone.

Traditional food photography requires:
- Food stylist ($400-800/day)
- Props and surfaces ($200-500)
- Specialized lighting for steam, gloss, and texture
- Multiple takes per dish
- Post-production compositing

**AI food photography changes this.** Take a quick phone photo of your dish, and AI handles the rest.

## The AI Food Photography Workflow

### Step 1: Capture a Base Photo
Use your phone. Here's the minimum-viable setup:
- Natural window light (side lighting preferred)
- White plate or bowl
- Clean table surface
- Phone steady on a stack of books

You don't need perfect. AI will enhance.

### Step 2: AI Enhancement
Upload to EcomPic AI. In one pass:
- **Background cleanup**: Remove table clutter, reflections, distracting elements
- **Color enhancement**: Boost saturation and contrast naturally, not artificially
- **Steam/sizzle effects**: Add subtle steam to hot dishes (delivery apps reward "hot and fresh" cues)
- **Lighting correction**: Even out shadows, brighten the hero ingredient

### Step 3: Platform-Specific Export
One enhanced photo → exports for every delivery platform. Properly sized, properly formatted, platform-compliant.

## Menu Photography Strategy

### Category Heroes
Photograph your top 3 sellers in each category. They anchor the menu visually and drive browsing.

### The "Anchor" Effect
A study by Grubhub found that meals with professional photos outsell non-photographed items by 28% — even when the photographed item is more expensive. Professional images create a quality halo across the entire menu.

### Seasonal Specials
Rotate images with seasonal menus. AI generates seasonal backgrounds and garnishes without re-shooting every dish.
