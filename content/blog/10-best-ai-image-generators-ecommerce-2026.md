## Why Generic AI Tools Fall Short for Ecommerce

**Midjourney** — Incredible creativity, but can't reliably preserve your specific product. Great for concept art, not for representing an actual SKU you're selling.

**DALL-E** — Strong at following prompts, but no product-specific features. No background removal. No batch processing.

**Stable Diffusion** — Open-source flexibility with plugins, but requires technical setup and doesn't guarantee product accuracy or platform compliance.

**Canva AI** — Good for social media templates, but not built for product photography at scale.

These tools are not competitors to ecommerce-specific generators — they're complementary. Use them for brand assets, social content, and creative direction. Use purpose-built tools for your product catalog.
