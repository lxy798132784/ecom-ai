## What Actually Matters for Sellers

If you're evaluating an AI image tool for your ecommerce business, look for these features:

### 1. Can It Handle White Backgrounds Properly?

This sounds simple. It's not. Amazon requires pure white (RGB 255,255,255). Many general-purpose AI tools leave a light gray or cream background that gets rejected.

EcomPic specifically targets Amazon-compliant white backgrounds. One click, done.

### 2. Does It Support Scene Generation?

White background images are step one. But the images that convert buyers are lifestyle shots—your product in a kitchen, living room, or outdoor setting.

A good tool should offer: kitchen, living room, bedroom, office, outdoor, bathroom, marble countertop, wooden table—at minimum.

### 3. What About Platform-Specific Sizes?

Amazon wants 2000×2000. eBay wants 1600×1600. Shopify likes 2048×2048. Temu is fine with 800×800. Your tool should let you export at the right size without opening Photoshop.

### 4. Can You Edit Existing Photos?

Sometimes you just need to change the color of your product, add a text overlay, or remove a reflection. Basic generation tools can't do this. Look for an "edit" or "transform" feature.

### 5. What's the True Cost?

Don't just look at monthly price. Calculate cost per usable image. Some tools generate 4 images per prompt but only 1 is usable—your effective cost is 4x higher.
