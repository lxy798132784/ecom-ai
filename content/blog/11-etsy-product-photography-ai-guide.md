# Etsy Product Photography Tips: AI-Powered Guide for Handmade Sellers

Etsy buyers shop with their eyes first. In a sea of handmade jewelry, custom furniture, and artisanal candles, your photos make the difference between a click and a scroll. Here's how to create Etsy photos that sell — powered by AI.

## Etsy Image Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| Format | JPEG, PNG, or GIF |
| Recommended size | 2000px on shortest side |
| Maximum file size | 20 MB |
| Number of slots | 10 per listing |
| Aspect ratio | 4:3, 3:2, or 5:4 recommended |
| Color space | sRGB |

Etsy recommends images at least 2000px for the zoom feature. Lower resolution images appear fuzzy when buyers zoom in to inspect details — and on Etsy, buyers zoom in.

## The 10-Image Etsy Strategy

### Image 1: Hero Shot (Best Photo)
Your absolute best photo. Buyers see this in search results. Make it stop the scroll — great lighting, clear product, on-brand aesthetic.

### Image 2: Alternate Angle
Different perspective. A ring from the side. A bag opened. A candle from above showing the wax pool.

### Image 3: Detail / Close-Up
Texture, craftsmanship, materials. Handmade products sell on quality — prove it with close-ups.

### Image 4: Scale Reference
Show size with common objects. A journal next to a coffee cup. Earrings on an ear. An art print framed on a wall.

### Image 5: In Use / Lifestyle
Show the product in its natural habitat. This is where AI lifestyle generation shines — drop your handmade ceramic mug into a cozy kitchen scene.

### Image 6: Packaging / Unboxing
Etsy buyers care about presentation. Show the packaging, tissue paper, thank-you note.

### Image 7: Customization Options
If you offer personalization, show examples. Monogrammed items. Color choices. Size comparisons.

### Image 8: Behind-the-Scenes
A photo of your workspace or process. Builds trust and justifies handmade pricing.

### Image 9: Styled Flat Lay
Overhead shot with complementary props. A candle with matches and dried flowers. Jewelry on a textured surface.

### Image 10: Video (or Seasonal Shot)
Etsy allows video in image slots. Or rotate seasonal images to keep listings fresh.

## How AI Helps Handmade Sellers

**Lifestyle scenes without the set.** AI drops your product into any setting. Your candle appears on a cozy winter mantel. Your necklace on a model's collarbone. Your art print on a styled gallery wall. No props, no studio, no model release forms.

**White background for clean hero shots.** Amazon isn't the only place that benefits from clean presentation. A white-background hero shot lets your product shine without distractions.

**Consistency across your shop.** AI applies the same quality, lighting, and framing to every product photo. Your shop looks professional and cohesive — even if you photographed items across different days, different rooms, different lighting.

**Seasonal refreshes without re-shooting.** Place your summer product lineup in autumn leaves. Your holiday collection in a snowy window scene. Update listings seasonally without a single new photo.

## Etsy Photography by Category

### Jewelry
- Macro close-ups showing metal finish and stone clarity
- On-model shots (ears, neck, wrist)
- Scale reference (coin or ruler)
- Lighting that shows sparkle without blowing out highlights

### Home Decor
- Room context showing scale
- Detail shots of material/texture
- Styled flat lay with complementary decor
- Before/after (if transformative)

### Art & Prints
- Framed on a wall, in multiple room styles
- Close-up showing paper texture
- Scale reference (person standing next to large piece)
- In-situ mockups generated by AI

### Clothing & Accessories
- On a model or dress form
- Fabric close-up showing weave/texture
- Color variants
- Care instructions visible

## Pro Tips for Etsy Success

- **Natural light looks best** for handmade goods — warm, inviting, authentic
- **Consistent backgrounds** across your shop create a premium brand feel
- **Seasonal updates** keep listings fresh in Etsy search algorithm
- **Video converts** — Etsy reports listings with video see higher engagement
- **Test your hero image** — small changes in composition can double click-through
